# On Dance

You need to listen to the beat.  I reflexively smiled after hearing that, and kept trying my best to hear this "beat" and move my body to it.  Hearing <that|accusation|phrase> didn't help what was going on internally in my brain, but I kept trying.  I'd love to just turn off my thoughts and just be with the vibe and have no worries; just be in the music, movement, and community.  However, I was a bit overwhelmed; I didn't know less than a minute ago I'd be dancing to the songs of a DJ on an empty dance floor with someone I don't know.  Not didn't know, don't know.  I still don't know this person, and that's okay; they definitely left an impact on me.  You see, after the first comment, we continued dancing a few feet away for a few more seconds before she repeated it.  You need to listen to the beat.  We both just stopped, looked at each other, then I put my head down and walked to the table with all my friends. "Charlie Browning" it back to the table.  After I took my seat, she came over to my friends and said "your friend needs to listen to the beat".  We all laughed and they gave me some more shit.  A minute earlier they all had outed me and sent me on this side quest.  The dancer had came to our table and asked us all "which one of ya'll are single?" Them all being couples unanimously pointed to me, the only single person.  She had me join her on the dance floor for that minute, not knowing that I would disappoint her and not be what she was looking for.  You can think this was a negative event, and it was, but it wasn't terrible; it's something I laughed about then and still do to this day.  It should be noted that dance is something memorable, whether negative or positive; you want it to be positive, but it can take effort and community to get there.  That event didn't discourage me, as I still went out and wiggled in clubs a few times since then.  It always stuck with me though; I like to improve, as I don't feel like I'm the person I'm meant to be yet.  It's not a pressure I put on myself though, instead I open to being conscious of my self (faults and not) and open to saying yes to things that feel like the next step in my life.  It wouldn't be until a decade after that event that I could say I'm getting close to dancing to the beat.

## The Journey

It's getting close to a year since I started partner dancing; as I write this, I'm going into my first summer of dance. If you're wondering if someone who has been doing something for year almost daily and writing about said something actually enjoys it or not, I love it. I absolutely fucking love dancing. I had no clue this is the hobby I would pick up and that I'd feel such joy and community. <find better segway here> My therapist has been pushing me to find a hobby where there is an in-person community to better align with my goals.  For the majority of my adult life, I have been behind a screen playing video games online with friends.  I'm responsible about it; I focused in school to get good grades, took advantage of internships in college, which lead to opportunities for my career. Doing all the stuff you gotta do to survive in this world. But on my free time, I often chose on-line video games in the comfort of my room. Logically, I knew I'm going to get the most out of life by being out in the community, but video games offered me a lot and it was a low bar to entry. Finding groups you really enjoy can take time and effort, and I have a group of friends from high school that I really enjoy goofing off in video games with.

I chose to start cutting back and spending more time in the real world -- waking up out of The Matrix yo. I'm the type that is content with just existing, and I'm thankful for anything extra.  I wasn't really enjoying some of the meet-ups I'd found, so I decided to do what people have been doing for centuries... I went to a local tavern.  I have a good selection of bars near me, but I found one that was worth the 5 minute walk to the 10 minute bus ride.  I'd hit that up almost every night when I was working from home; I'd sit at the bar, watch what they'd have on the TV, periodically play on my phone, try to be a delightful guest to the staff, order drinks and the occasional food.
